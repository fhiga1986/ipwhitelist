What is IP geolocation?

IP geolocation is the process of determining the real-world geographic location of an IP address. Every public IP address is assigned to a specific organization (ISP, company, or cloud provider) in a specific region. By cross-referencing IP allocation databases, geolocation services can pinpoint an IP address to a city, region, and country with reasonable accuracy.

How accurate is IP geolocation?

IP geolocation is typically accurate to the country level (99%+ accuracy) and city level (50-80% accuracy). It is not precise enough to identify a street address. The location shown is usually the location of your ISP's nearest point of presence, not your exact physical location. If you use a VPN, the location shown will be the VPN server's location, not yours.

What can developers use IP geolocation for?

  • Security — Detecting logins from unusual locations or blocking traffic from specific regions
  • Content localization — Serving region-appropriate content, currency, or language
  • Fraud detection — Flagging transactions from high-risk regions
  • Analytics — Understanding where your users are located
  • Compliance — Restricting access based on geographic regulations (GDPR, export controls)

FAQ

IP location questions

Why does my IP location show the wrong city?
▾
IP geolocation shows the location of your ISP's infrastructure, not your physical location. Your ISP may route traffic through a hub in a different city. Additionally, if you use a VPN, the location shown is the VPN server's location. City-level accuracy varies between 50-80% depending on the geolocation database used.
Can I look up any IP address location?
▾
Yes — enter any public IPv4 address in the lookup field above. Private IP addresses (192.168.x.x, 10.x.x.x, 172.16.x.x) cannot be geolocated as they are local network addresses not routable on the public internet.

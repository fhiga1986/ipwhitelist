What to include in a bug report

  • The tool and the exact input you used (domain, IP, ports). Please don't include anything private — the issue tracker is public.
  • What you expected and what you got instead. A screenshot helps.
  • Your browser and OS — the user agent checker has a Copy button for exactly this.
  • Whether you are on a VPN or a corporate network, since several tools measure things from your connection.

Requesting a tool or a cloud provider

Missing a config format in the whitelist generator (Azure NSG, Cloudflare WAF, Supabase, Railway…) or a diagnostic you need often? Tell me what you do today by hand and how often — the most-requested additions are built first.
